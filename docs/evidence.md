## Interpretation rules

- An “observed by” date confirms a state exists by that screenshot, not its exact completion date.
- The January system cards and February global header are different snapshots. Do not force their numbers to reconcile.
- Roster assignments remain confirmed in the latest checkpoint. Hold values come from an older detailed roster and are labelled historical.
- The map uses illustrative positions. Connections represent campaign relationships; they are not verified astronomical coordinates, distances or calculated SCUT links.
- Request credit can occur on dispatch. Physical delivery must be separately verified.
- No deployed state is inferred from an open confirmation dialog.
