# Expedition 001 — establish the first independent industrial foothold

This is a documented campaign route, not a claim of an optimal build. Exact costs and tutorial requirements can change; read the quotes in your current game. These instructions have not yet been independently tested by another player.

## Outcome

Establish local production and communications at Tau Ceti using a Surveyor/Builder seed pair, while retaining home command and resident support operations. Success means the pair has actually arrived, mining/refining are working and a SCUT relay is online. A dispatched probe is not a completed foothold.

## 1. Establish the home baseline

Pause. Record the game date, local ore/parts, mining equipment, factory queues, research and tutorial progress. Our recorded baseline was July 29, 2152, at Epsilon Eridani.

Start mining and complete the tutorial requirements shown in your game. Our early sequence included a second roamer, SUDDAR/surveys and Engines I. Capture the displayed clone unlock requirements and price; do not use an upgraded older campaign's quotes.

## 2. Match industry to supply

Create a resident Refiner when local ore accumulation justifies one. In this run, Mary remained at Epsilon Eridani in HOME-INDUSTRY beside Holmes in HOME-COMMAND. Add refining capacity against an observed backlog rather than assuming more miners are always the priority.

Survey completion reveals resources and objects; individual object scans are a separate action. Record actual salvage messages and dates rather than infer rewards from a finished survey bar.

## 3. Test resident support through beaming

When beaming is offered, inspect the current source, destination, specialist type, cost and duration. Our Octavia beam to Sol ran August 10–18, 2158. Observations confirmed a factory, one roamer and one refinery with zero observed starting stock.

Mae was later established at Alpha Centauri, but her exact beam quote and arrival date were not captured. These two beamed footholds became productive before their support freighters arrived. Do not assume that proves the same starter equipment for every destination or physical arrival.

## 4. Prepare SEED-01

Our team was Banneker / Surveyor and Katherine / Builder, grouped SEED-01. Both departed Epsilon Eridani for Tau Ceti with 50 parts each. Banneker left August 19, 2162; Katherine left September 23. The displayed initial flight estimate was 10.9 years.

Recheck cargo and origin before confirming each departure. Selecting another resident changes the departure origin and can result in an unintended multi-decade flight. Keep the resident helpers at their assigned systems.

The 100 combined parts were a startup budget, not proof of a fixed required loadout. The earlier relay quote was 80 parts and AMI manager 60, so those two purchases together exceeded that cargo. Confirm destination production and actual prices before choosing the build sequence.

## 5. Establish Tau Ceti

On arrival, pause before upgrades and record factory, equipment, starting stock and carried cargo. Our immediate physical-arrival package was not captured, so this record cannot specify it precisely.

Start SUDDAR and mining, inspect local refining, and build/place the required communications equipment using actual quotes. By June 14, 2174, both seeds were working at Tau Ceti with a factory, two mining roamers, one refinery and a SCUT relay. The Oort survey completion is logged July 10, 2174. Individual objects were still unscanned in that later sequence.

## 6. Verify the result and record lessons

Capture the roster, system inventory, map and flight list. Record what was planned, what actually happened, and any change to the next mission. Keep local stored inventory separate from hold cargo, and keep dispatch, request credit and physical delivery separate.

The seed pair remains at Tau Ceti at the current February 2184 checkpoint. A resident handoff and onward expedition have not been executed. Delta Eridani and Gliese 54 are future beam missions for another session.
